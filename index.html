The company

We build the intelligence layer for industries that cannot afford to guess.

Swiss Tech builds intelligent software for two industries where a delayed decision has a physical cost: a patient waiting on a diagnosis, and a shipment waiting on a dock. Both run on the same raw material — fragmented operational data — and both have been served for decades by software that only records what already happened.

We start from the opposite end. Every Swiss Tech product is designed around a model that reads the operation continuously, surfaces what deserves attention, and takes the routine work off the people doing the skilled work. Enterprise-grade in the ways that actually matter: auditable, isolated per tenant, and answerable at 3am.

Ask a question

How long does a deployment actually take?

A scoped single-site deployment reaches live users in six weeks: one week discovery, one week design, three weeks build on your own data, one week staged rollout. Multi-site and custom integration work extends the build phase, not the others — we'll give you the honest number after discovery, not before it.

Where does our data live, and who can see it?

Each tenant gets an isolated data store in the region you nominate. Encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access with least privilege, and a full audit trail on every read and write. Our engineers require a time-boxed, logged elevation to access production data, and you can require your own approval on that. On-premise deployment is available where regulation demands it.

Is our data used to train models for other customers?

No. Tenant data trains tenant models only. Architectural improvements are shared across deployments; your data is not. This is contractual, not a policy we can quietly revise.

Can it integrate with the system we already have?

Healthcare: HL7 v2, FHIR R4, DICOM metadata, and flat-file import for older systems. Logistics: EDI X12/EDIFACT, REST and webhook ingestion, plus direct telematics feeds. Where no interface exists we build an adapter during the develop phase — that scope is priced in discovery, never discovered late.

What happens when the AI is wrong?

Every prediction is a recommendation with a confidence score and the features behind it — a human always holds the decision. Overrides are captured as training signal, so disagreement improves the model rather than being discarded. No clinical or dispatch action executes autonomously unless you explicitly configure it to.

How is it priced?

An annual platform fee by deployment scale, plus per-seat licensing for clinical and operational users. Integration and custom model work are quoted once after discovery — fixed, not time-and-materials. No usage-based inference billing, because unpredictable bills make people avoid using the product.
